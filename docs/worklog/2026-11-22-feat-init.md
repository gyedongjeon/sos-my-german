## Request

Start `sos-my-german`, the German version of `sos-my-english`, as the first branch of the
repository, using that project as the reference. Every feature of `sos-my-english` was to be
brought over (not only the shared base), and the partner's voice was to come from Piper, since
Kokoro has no German voice.

## Changes

- **Copied from `sos-my-english` v0.6.1**, then renamed: `SOS My German`, `sos-my-german`, the
  `SOS_MY_GERMAN_` environment variables, the app id, the update repository, and the release
  asset names. The version starts again at `0.1.0` in all three packages. The worklogs and the
  screenshots of `sos-my-english` were left out; screenshots of this app can follow once it has
  been used.
- **What stays in English**: the UI, the built-in topics' titles and situations, the pattern
  formulas' slot names ("+ infinitive"), and the titles of topics made from a text. What the
  learner hears, says, and is corrected on is German.
- **Speech-to-text**: Whisper's multilingual `small` model (~480 MB) in place of `small.en`, told
  that the speech is German (`language="de"`).
- **Text-to-speech: Piper** (`piper-tts` 1.8) in place of `kokoro-onnx`, with the voice
  `de_DE-thorsten-high` (~114 MB) from `rhasspy/piper-voices`, pinned to its `v1.0.0` release.
  `TTS_VOICE` now names any Piper voice (`de_DE-kerstin-low`); its two files are downloaded into
  `data/tts/` and loaded once per voice. The cache, the background download with progress, and
  the browser-voice fallback (now `de-DE`) work as before. Alternatives considered: the
  browser's voice alone (quality depends on the system), and keeping Kokoro (no German voice).
  `piper-tts` is GPL-3.0; it is downloaded on the first launch like the other Python packages
  and is not in the repository or the installer, which the README says.
- **Prompts**: the partner, the corrections, the review, the opening lines, topics from a text,
  the pattern drills and checks, video levels, own patterns, and the searches for similar videos
  are all written for German. The partner is told to say "du" or "Sie" as the situation calls for
  and to keep to it. Every text written for the learner (a correction's explanation, a pattern's
  note and verdict, the review) is asked for in the learner's own language "whatever language the
  conversation was in", and the review prompt names that language at its start: in live runs the
  model sometimes wrote the review, and once a pattern verdict, in German, though Korean was
  chosen. The review's phrase field is `german` (was `english`), in the API and in
  the UI. With German as the feedback language, a pattern sentence is shown as a short German
  instruction.
- **Built-in content**: the 40 topics have German opening lines. The 100 sentence patterns
  (20 A1, 20 A2, 20 B1, 20 B2, 12 C1, 8 C2) were written anew for German, with Profile deutsch
  and the Goethe-Zertifikat syllabuses as the reference, for example "Ich möchte + noun /
  + infinitive", "hätte + infinitive + sollen", and "Kaum hatte + subject + past participle,
  als …".
- **Words as heard** (corrections practice, shadowing, and the pattern check's shortcut): the
  English short forms ("I'm", "won't") are gone; "geht's" counts as "geht es", "ß" as "ss" (as
  Swiss German and some transcripts write it), and German number words up to "zwanzig" and the
  tens (and "zwo") as figures.
- **Subtitles**: German tracks (`de`, `de-DE`, `de-AT`, `de-CH`) are asked for; German
  abbreviations ("z.B.", "Dr.", "ca.", "usw.", …) do not end a sentence; stage directions with
  umlauts ("(Gelächter)") are left out like "(Beifall)".
- **Search** of the saved subtitles ignores the case of umlauts too ("ERZÄHLEN" finds
  "erzählen"); SQLite's `LIKE` ignored the case of ASCII letters only.
- **App icon**: the same life ring and speech-bubble tail on a charcoal tile, with the tail on the
  right, so the app can be told apart from `sos-my-english` (navy, tail on the left) in the Dock.
- **Tests** whose behaviour depends on the language (word matching, shadowing, subtitles,
  pattern mistakes, the review's field, the voice, the Whisper model) were rewritten with German
  sentences, and new ones cover "ß"/"ss", German abbreviations and stage directions, umlauts in
  the search, and German recognition. The ten live tests send German sentences.
- **CI runs on every push to a pull request** (`synchronize` added to `.github/workflows/ci.yml`),
  not only when it is opened: a fix pushed to this pull request was not tested until the run was
  started by hand. A push cancels the run still going for the previous one, as before.
- **Docs**: `README.md` and `README.ko.md` describe the German app (sizes, models, examples,
  matching rules, the Windows state, Piper's licence); `AGENTS.md` names the project.
